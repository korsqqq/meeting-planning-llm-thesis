# Crossover and interaction power — results

Computed 2026-08-27 by `scripts/analyse_interaction_power.py` (committed at
`a8e683a`, before this output existed) under `PRE_ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` as amended
the same day. Development data only. No held-out seed was read; the loader
aborts on any instance whose seed reaches the held-out floor.

Settings: 200 nested-resampling draws, 200 replicates each, 299 permutations per
test, seed 20260827, α = 0.05.

---

## 1. The registered decision rule is not satisfied

> smallest `N ∈ {50, 60, 65}` with 10th-percentile power ≥ 0.80 for **both** the
> IUT and `Δ`, at effect scale 1.0, after Holm across the two primary tests

**No `N` in the grid satisfies it.** The rule is recorded as failed rather than
adjusted.

Effect scale 1.0, after Holm, median [10th–90th percentile]:

| `N` | IUT (crossover) | `Δ` (High − Low) |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 0.985 [**0.600**, 1.000] | 1.000 [0.980, 1.000] |
| 60 | 0.995 [**0.655**, 1.000] | 1.000 [1.000, 1.000] |
| 65 | 0.998 [**0.697**, 1.000] | 1.000 [1.000, 1.000] |

The binding constraint is the IUT's 10th percentile, and nothing else. `Δ` is not
close to binding at any `N` or any scale. Neither is the median — reading point
estimates would have declared `N = 50` comfortable at 0.985. The plan required the
10th percentile precisely so that this could not happen.

Holm costs almost nothing: raw and corrected IUT power are identical in every
cell, and the largest correction cost anywhere is 0.005 on `Δ` at scale 0.5.

## 2. `N` is not the lever

Going from 50 to 65 — a 30% increase in held-out GPU time — moves the binding
number from 0.600 to 0.697 and still does not reach 0.80.

That is not a shortage of held-out instances. The 10th percentile is generated by
the **outer** resampling, which asks what the power would be if the true effect
sat at the unlucky end of what **20 development observations per band** can
resolve. With sd ≈ 0.62 on 20 observations the standard error of the band mean is
≈ 0.14, so the 10th-percentile pseudo-population carries a mean near 0.20 instead
of 0.379 — roughly half the effect. Increasing `N` shrinks sampling error *within*
a replicate; it does nothing to the uncertainty about which effect size is true.
This is the concrete form of the plan's own warning that resampling 20 points up
to 65 redistributes information rather than creating any.

The lever that would move this number is more **development** data, not a larger
held-out experiment.

## 3. Effect-size sensitivity

IUT power, 10th percentile, after Holm:

| scale | `N` = 50 | 60 | 65 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | 0.600 | 0.655 | 0.697 |
| 0.75 | 0.544 | 0.540 | 0.706 |
| 0.50 | 0.374 | 0.434 | 0.450 |

If the true crossover is half the size of the observed main effect, the design is
clearly underpowered for it at every `N` considered (median 0.802–0.897, floor
0.374–0.450).

The non-monotonicity at scale 0.75 (60 below 50) is estimation noise, not a
property of the design — see §6.

## 4. The implementation is calibrated

Level under the least-favourable nulls:

| `N` | IUT raw | IUT Holm | `Δ` raw | `Δ` Holm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0450 | 0.0200 |
| 60 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0500 | 0.0200 |
| 65 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0450 | 0.0250 |

`Δ`'s label permutation sits on nominal 0.05 at every `N`, which is what an exact
test under exchangeability should do and is the main evidence that the machinery
is right. The IUT's near-zero level is also correct rather than suspicious: at the
least-favourable point both components sit on their boundary and both must reject,
so the joint rate is ≈ α². That conservatism is the price of a claim about two
directions at once, and it is paid in power, not in validity.

## 5. Sensitivity analysis, and the assumption audit

The sign-test reconstruction of the IUT (sensitivity only, per amendment A1 — a
different functional, not a rival test of the same one) is uniformly weaker and
much wider: 0.885 [0.298, 1.000] at `N` = 50, scale 1.0. It agrees on direction
and on the ordering across `N` and scale, and it does not change any conclusion.

**Skewness of the observed paired differences: −0.47 (low), −0.53 (medium), −0.46
(high).** These are not symmetric. The sign-flip reference distribution is exact
for symmetry about zero, so the primary test's exactness rests on an assumption
the data mildly violates in all three bands, in the same direction. This is
reported, not repaired — the assumption was declared in amendment A3 for exactly
this reason.

One property of the construction follows from it: negating the low band to build
the crossover alternative also flips its skew, so the assumed alternative has the
two bands skewed in opposite directions. Sign reversal preserves the lattice, the
magnitudes and the tie mass, but not the direction of asymmetry.

## 6. Limits of these numbers

* Each `(scale, N)` cell draws its own independent set of 200 pseudo-populations.
  Comparisons *across* `N` therefore carry outer noise, which is why scale 0.75 is
  non-monotone. The headline — that no cell reaches 0.80 — does not depend on
  this, but the "+0.097 from 50 to 65" figure is itself noisy and should not be
  extrapolated.
* `N` outside {50, 60, 65} was not computed. The plan restricted the grid and
  said so; extrapolating a threshold `N` from three noisy points would be
  inventing a number.
* The crossover alternative is assumed, not observed. The development data
  contains no crossover in any band. These figures describe the design's ability
  to detect a crossover of a stipulated size, and are not evidence that one exists.

## 7. What this leaves open

The registered rule did not select an `N`, so the choice is not settled by this
computation and requires a decision plus a dated amendment before any held-out
seed is generated. The relevant facts:

* `N` = 65 costs about 30% more GPU time than `N` = 50 and still fails the
  criterion, so the extra cost buys no certification of the crossover claim.
* At `N` = 50 the crossover test has median power 0.985 with a 10th-percentile
  floor of 0.600, and `Δ` has power ≥ 0.98 throughout.
* Amendment A2 made the medium band descriptive on grounds of internal
  consistency, before this computation was run. A side effect, visible only now,
  is that medium was the only band that pushed the **main-effect** requirement
  above 50 (perm power 0.741 at `N` = 50, against 0.983 low and 1.000 high in
  `../empirical_power/`). With medium descriptive, `N` = 50 clears 0.80 in both
  confirmatory bands for the main effect.
